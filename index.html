Kuicur Sonic Toothbrush

Scroll, or press the button on the handle.

The head

Soft, flexible, U‑shaped

Bristles set at 45% greater density and cut to follow the curve of the teeth. The head clips onto the metal shaft, and ten of them come in the box — about thirty months at one every three.

One button

Press the white button

Press once to start. Press again for the next mode. Hold it two to three seconds to stop, or let it run past the last mode and it stops on its own.

Cleaning

Every day

“For normal, everyday cleaning.” The one you leave it on: two minutes, twice a day, at full speed.

Bright White

For stains

“For deep cleaning and stain removal.” Aimed at the coffee and tea marks on the faces you can see.

Polishing

For the front

“For polishing front teeth.” For the six front teeth anyone actually sees.

Nursing

For gums

“For gentle gum care.” The gentle one for the gum line, where brushing usually goes wrong.

Cleansing

For sensitive teeth

“For sensitive teeth and gums.” The gentlest setting. Start here if hot and cold make you flinch.

Charge

The last mark

The bar at the foot of the column is the battery. Behind it sits a 1500 mAh cell, good for about ninety days — and the ring around the button lights while it charges, so you can read it across the room.
